First public release of CounterSignal, performance monitoring for deployed clinical risk models that corrects for the interventions their alerts trigger.

## Included

* Monitor with standard, untreated only and inverse probability weighted views of observed/expected, AUC, calibration intercept and slope and Brier score, plus a doubly robust observed/expected.
* Monthly verdicts (stable, drift, not enough evidence yet) from 95% bootstrap intervals, with an estimate of the sample needed to settle open cases.
* Two sided CUSUM alarm with exact small sample standardization and a simulated control limit.
* Population stability index early signals for months awaiting outcomes.
* Plain language report card, Streamlit dashboard and a page for running the monitor on your own CSV files.
* UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals loader with checksum verification, and a semi-synthetic benchmark.

## Benchmark highlights

On 69,973 real encounters with simulated alerts and treatment (200 deployments per scenario): with the model unchanged, standard monitoring raised a false alarm in 96.5% of deployments and CounterSignal in 5.5% (planned 5%). When readmissions rose among alerted patients, CounterSignal caught the change in 97.5% of deployments, against 57.0% for standard monitoring and 65.0% for untreated only monitoring. Details and limits: docs/benchmark.md.

## Known limits

The corrected estimates assume every driver of treatment is logged and every kind of patient has some chance of going untreated. Reweighting widens monthly intervals; at 2,000 patients a month most single months return "not enough evidence yet".
